# The ground lattice, orbited down to a flat camera.
#
# The floor is ruled over the box round the four corners of the window cast
# back onto Z = 0.  Tip the camera towards the ground and that box grows
# without limit, which is why there has always been a cap - and the cap used
# to mean a low view got no floor at all.
#
# The pitch is now coarsened per direction until the lines are far enough
# apart to read, so a flat view rules a coarse floor instead of none, and an
# ordinary view rules about ninety lines where it used to rule two hundred
# and sixty.  This drives it from overhead down to nearly flat, which is the
# range where both the cap and the coarsening have to behave.
#
# What to look for: hsk-og-high has an even lattice under the model;
# hsk-og-low still has a floor, coarser, with nothing crawling or banding.
activate
wait 1200
key slash
type timings
key return
wait 500
key slash
type 3d
key return
wait 1200
shot /tmp/hsk-og-high.png

key o
wait 400
at 700 300
press
at 700 420 400
at 700 540 400
at 700 660 400
release
wait 900
shot /tmp/hsk-og-low.png
