#

# make room for the toys knobs and the issue is that line of the smaller
# inner rectangle is not staying snapped... sketchup doesnt seem to detach it

# makes it to the outer edges of its bigger parent rectangle"
#
# What to look for: hsk-move-grab has the one side lit and nothing else;
# hsk-move-drag is the halfway picture, where the two sides it joins should
# be leaning over to follow rather than the side floating free; hsk-move-done
# is after the click - the inner rectangle should be a rectangle still, one
# side shorter, and that side the same length it started.

# and the other way: /detach on, after which a move takes the side away on
# its own and leaves the two it was joined to where they are.  SketchUp has
# no such thing; Tony asked for both.  hsk-move-loose is the ghost, which
# should be amber with no leaning edges behind it, and hsk-move-alone is
# after the click - the inner rectangle left open and the side loose in it.

key z
