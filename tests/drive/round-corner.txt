# Rounding a corner off: the whole reason edges have to break where they
# cross.
#
# Tony, 15 September: "this is how i make rounded corners in a rectangle.  i
# use the circle tool and temporary lines.  in sketchup i would be able to
# remove all of those lines individually because the circle would have broke
# the lines making the point in sketchup... then i would have been able to
# delete them individually and delete the inner left over piece of circle."
#
# Before this the rectangle's side was still one line corner to corner and
# the circle was still one closed loop, so there was nothing to rub out but
# the whole of each.
#
# What to look for: hsk-round-drawn has a rectangle with a circle sitting on
# its bottom right corner; hsk-round-hover shows the eraser lighting only the
# short piece of the side between the touch point and the corner, not the
# whole side; hsk-round-done has that piece and three quarters of the circle
# gone, leaving the corner rounded off.

geometry 0 0 1500 850
activate
wait 2500
key escape
wait 600

# flat on the floor, so what is drawn is what is clicked
key slash
type plan
key return
wait 1200

key r
wait 300
at 500 300
click l
wait 300
at 1000 620
click l
wait 900
shot /tmp/hsk-round-rect.png

# a circle tucked into the bottom right corner, touching both sides
key escape
key c
wait 300
at 920 540
click l
wait 300
at 1000 540
click l
wait 1200
shot /tmp/hsk-round-drawn.png

key escape
key e
wait 400
# the short piece of the bottom side, between the touch point and the corner
at 960 620
wait 900
shot /tmp/hsk-round-hover.png
click l
wait 600
# the short piece of the right side
at 1000 580
wait 500
click l
wait 900
shot /tmp/hsk-round-done.png
