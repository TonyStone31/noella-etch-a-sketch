# A face is what a closed run of edges encloses.
#
# Tony, 15 September: "in SketchUp I don't think you can even have a filled
# face unless it is enclosed by lines.  So when I am erasing lines on a cube
# it will leave behind faces and I think that is wrong.  I think also when I
# delete a face in SketchUp let's say in a cube there is a way to put it back
# if I remember correctly but it was a pain in the ass."
#
# He is right on both counts and SketchUp says so in as many words - "faces
# are erased when you erase their bounding edges", and "Undo, or redraw the
# line that was removed - the face comes back on its own".
#
# A loose face got the first for free: they are all thrown away and worked
# out again from the edges whenever anything changes.  A built solid's faces
# are kept as they were made, so rubbing an edge off a box left six sides
# standing with nothing under one of them.
#
# What to look for: hsk-fne-box is the box; hsk-fne-erased has the top front
# edge gone and with it the top and the front wall, so you are looking into
# an open box; hsk-fne-healed has the edge traced back in with the line tool
# and the two sides back with it.

geometry 0 0 1500 850
activate
wait 2500

wait 600
