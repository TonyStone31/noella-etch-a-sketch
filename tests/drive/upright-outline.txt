# Drawing a closed outline standing up, in the 3D view.
#
# Tony, 13 September: "the reason I drew it laying down is because I had to
# draw all those lines on the same axis so they became a face.  And the
# problem with trying to draw that shape while it was in 3D view is the
# stupid cursor kept trying to go to the other axes."
#
# The working plane's offset came from the cursor, so one inference off the
# plane took the plane with it and nothing after that was coplanar - the
# outline never closed and no face was ever made.  Held to the first point of
# the shape now, and an explicitly locked plane beats every inference.
#
# What this proves: press L, press Left to stand the plane up, click five
# corners and close it, and there is a face.  Which is the whole of what he
# was trying to do and had to go and do flat on the floor instead.

geometry 0 0 1500 850
activate
wait 800

key escape
key l
wait 300
# left arrow: upright, on the XZ plane
key left
wait 400
shot /tmp/hsk-upright-hint.png

at 700 500
click
wait 250
at 820 470
click
wait 250
at 860 330
click
wait 250
at 800 300
click
wait 250
at 760 420
click
wait 250
# back to the start, which closes it
at 700 500
click
wait 1000
key escape
wait 500
shot /tmp/hsk-upright.png
