# The house model opens and looks like an etch-a-sketch.
#
# What this proves: examples/etch-a-sketch.hsk loads, the solids are closed
# so nothing shows a blue back, and the robot drawn on the screen is there as
# stylus lines waiting for somebody to push one of its squares.
