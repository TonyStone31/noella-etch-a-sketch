# Push/pull's stipple has to stop at the window in the face.
#
# From a note, 15 September, building the toy's case: "using the push/pull tool and
# when i am hovering over the outer ring face it highlights the face
# including the smaller rectangle face inside!  it should only be
# highlighting as much of the face as it can see!"
#
# A rectangle drawn inside another leaves a ring - an outline with a window
# in it.  The push itself has always known that, and so has the area readout
# and the eraser's wash; only push/pull's hover said otherwise, because it
# was clipping the dots to the outline and never asking about the holes.
#
# What to look for: hsk-ring-hover has the stipple on the border only, with
# the inner rectangle clean and a bold edge round both loops, and the face
# reading in the top right saying the area of the ring rather than the whole
# rectangle.  hsk-ring-inner is the same tool over the middle, where the
# inner face gets the stipple and the border stays clean.
