# A picture in the manual, clicked, opens larger in a window of its own.
#
# Tony: "for the gif files... be able to click them and see a larger image".
# Every picture is a link to itself; in the program a click opens the
# picture window, fitted to its size, and Esc closes it again.
#
# run-drive.sh puts a copy of docs/help beside the program first.
#
# What to look for: hsk-pic-1 is the Push/Pull page with its animation;
# hsk-pic-2 is the animation filling a window of its own; hsk-pic-3 is back
# on the Push/Pull page, which the click did not replace.
activate
wait 1200
key slash
type manual
key return
wait 2500
at 215 662 500
click
wait 1800
shot /tmp/hsk-pic-1.png
at 600 705 400
click
wait 2500
shot /tmp/hsk-pic-2.png
key escape
wait 800
shot /tmp/hsk-pic-3.png
