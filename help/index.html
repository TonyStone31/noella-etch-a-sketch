About this help

Every picture is taken on an empty sheet, with the smallest thing that shows the tool and nothing else in the frame. A page about the circle tool wants a circle, not a circle drawn across somebody else's robot.

They are taken by driving the program itself rather than by anybody posing for them - tools/help-shots.txt is the script, and adding one is three lines.

The words are right everywhere. The pictures are arriving: a handful are taken, the rest are still marked "not taken yet" on the page that wants them. shots/NEEDED.md lists every one and what should be in it - and how they are taken, which is by driving the program itself rather than by anybody posing for them.

In a hurry? The keyboard and the mouse is one page with every key on it, and Commands is every slash command, grouped by what you are trying to do.
