The plan view

Looking straight down, and cutting through.

/plan, or I to toggle between plan and isometric.

Looking straight down

PLAN is the view that prints like a drawing: no perspective, no foreshortening, and a scale that means something. Drawing is easier here too - what you click is where it lands, with no plane to think about.

Cutting through

A plan is a slice, not a view from above. Set a cut height and only what lies inside it is drawn - so a wall shows as two lines and the roof over it does not.

  • /cut 0 9' - sets the bottom and the top of the slice.
  • /cut all - opens it right up to the whole model.
  • /cut off - turns the slice off.
  • Ctrl+wheel over the drawing travels up and down through the model, carrying the slice with it. Easier than typing heights, because you can watch it.
  • Right-click a floor and choose Plan From Here - one click instead of two numbers.

What you get

Anything below the slice is drawn faintly and dashed, the way a drawing shows a beam over a door or a footing under a wall. Anything above it is not drawn at all.

Printing it

PLAN is the view to print from, and to export SVG from - see printing.
