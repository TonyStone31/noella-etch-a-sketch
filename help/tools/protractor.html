Protractor

Lay a guide line at an angle.

No letter of its own - /protractor, or /angle, or pick it from MORE TOOLS.

How to use it

  1. Click the corner the angle turns about.
  2. Click a point to measure the angle from - along an edge is easiest.
  3. Swing round and click, or type the angle and press Enter.

It leaves a guide line at that angle. Draw against it, then rub it out.

Typing the angle

  • 45 or 22.5 - degrees.
  • 8:12 - a slope, eight in twelve, the way a roof pitch is written on a drawing. Use it and the arithmetic is never yours to get wrong.

Which plane it turns in

The arrow keys pick it before you start, and the color of the protractor says which - left or right for upright, up or down for flat. Without that it uses whatever plane your three points happened to lie in.

Worth knowing

The guide it leaves is a thing you can pick: select it and press Delete, or right-click anywhere and choose Clear Guides to be rid of the lot. See the tape measure for the rest of what guides do.
