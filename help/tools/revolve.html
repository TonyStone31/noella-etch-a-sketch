Revolve

Spin a face round an axis into a solid.

No letter of its own - /revolve, or pick it from the strip on the left.

This is SketchUp's Follow Me under a name that says what it does. /followme still works if that is the word you know.

Spinning a profile

  1. Draw the outline of half of the thing, seen edge on - the profile. For a wine glass that is the stem and the bowl in section, standing up, with a straight side down the middle where the axis will be.
  2. Click the face.
  3. Click two points on the axis to spin it about. The straight side of the profile is nearly always the one you want.

The ring the preview draws is what it is about to sweep. Red means the axis cuts through the outline, which cannot make a solid - move the axis off the shape, or use the straight side.

Sweeping along a path

Click a circle instead of an axis and the face follows that circle round - which is how a ring, a torus or a round handrail gets made. Any closed outline works as the path.

A part turn

Type an angle before the last click and it stops there: 90 for a quarter, 180 for a half. A pipe elbow is a circle swept a quarter turn about an axis off to one side of it.

How round it comes out

As round as the profile is. A revolve made from a twelve-sided circle has twelve facets; set the sides higher before you draw it - + and -, or type 48s - and it comes out smooth. See the circle tool.
