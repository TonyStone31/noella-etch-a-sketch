# Follow Me
Source: https://help.sketchup.com/en/sketchup/extruding-follow-me  (fetched 2 Sep 2026)

Extrudes a profile face along a path.  Crown moulding, spindles, bowls — and for
us, a duct running a route.

## Preselected path (the reliable way)
1. Draw the profile face, roughly **perpendicular to the path**.
2. Select the continuous run of edges that make the path.
3. Pick Follow Me.
4. Click the profile.  It extrudes along the selection.

## By hand
Pick Follow Me, then click and **drag the profile face along the path**, keeping
the pointer touching the path.  Click at the end.  Esc restarts.

## Lathing
Draw a circle.  Draw a profile — a cutaway of half the final shape — perpendicular
to the circle with its base on the circle's center.  Select the circle, pick
Follow Me, click the profile.

If it fails, zoom in and look for gaps in the profile; close them with the Line
tool.

Profile and path must be in the same group.
