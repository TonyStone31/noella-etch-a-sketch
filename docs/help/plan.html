The plan view

Looking straight down, and cutting through.

  • Switch the view to PLAN.
  • Set a cut height and only what is inside that slice is drawn.
  • Ctrl and the wheel over the drawing moves the slice up and down.
  • Right-click a floor and choose Plan From Here.
