The entity panel

What is picked, and the few things you can change about it.

/info puts it down the right-hand side. /info off takes it away. It is remembered between sessions.

It reads whatever is picked, and changes as you pick something else - which is why it is docked rather than a dialog you have to open.

With one thing picked

PickedIt says
A linelength, both ends, pen width, whether the crease is softened, and which solid it belongs to
A circle or an arcradius, center, sweep, how many sides, which plane it was drawn in
A facearea, how many corners, how many windows, which solid
A notewhat it says, where it points, its size
A dimensionwhat it measures, and what is written on it if that was typed over
A guidewhere it is

What you can change from it

  • Sides, on a circle or an arc - - and + beside the figure. This is the one worth knowing: the number of sides used to be something you set before drawing and could never change afterwards. Draw a circle at 24, decide it wants to be 48, and it is two presses.
  • Soften, on a line or an arc. A softened crease is hidden except where the surface turns away from you, which is what makes a pulled circle read as a pipe rather than a barrel of staves.
  • Size, on a note.
  • Reverse, on a face - or on several at once. See faces, front and back.

Everything it changes goes on the undo stack like anything else.

With several picked

How many of each, the total length of the lines and the total area of the faces - and a Reverse button if there are faces among them. Nothing else is editable with more than one thing picked, on purpose: a stepper that acted on nine things at once is a way to lose nine things.

With nothing picked

What the sheet adds up to: how many things, how many lines and how long they are altogether, how many faces and how much area. It is a question people ask of a drawing often enough to be worth the space when there is nothing else to show.
