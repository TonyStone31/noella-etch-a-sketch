Exporting

Getting the drawing out - pictures, film, and shapes.

  • PNG and JPEG - a picture at any size, or one of the ready-made sizes for somewhere you are going to post it. A PNG can have nothing behind it.
  • GIF - a little film that swings round the model. See recording a move.
  • SVG - the lines of this view as vectors, at true size. The file says how many inches or millimetres across it is, so it opens at the right size in a drawing program, a print shop, or a cutting machine, with nothing to scale by hand. Export from a plan and the size on the paper is the size of the thing.
  • DXF - this view flat, or the model in three dimensions, for somebody else's CAD.
  • STL - triangles in millimetres, which is what a 3D printer wants. It tells you whether the shape is closed.
  • OpenSCAD - a polyhedron per solid, to cut and union in OpenSCAD.
  • STL and OpenSCAD arrive centred on the origin, so a slicer opens them where it expects to.
