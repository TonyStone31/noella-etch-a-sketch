This program and the others

What this is for, and when to go and use something else.

There is no shortage of drawing programs, and some of them are very good at things this one does not attempt. A manual that pretends otherwise is not much use to somebody deciding whether to bother, so here is the honest version. The longer table, with links and dates, is in the repository.

What this is for

Roughing something out at a real scale, quickly, and getting a drawing or a file out the other end. A duct transition, a run of pipe, a shed, a bracket, the shape of a room. Pick a scale, put the cursor on a point, type the size, and print it at true scale or export a DXF.

Two things make it worth choosing over the program most people would reach for. It runs on Linux, which SketchUp has never done. And the trade work is in the box - fittings from your own measurements, a pipe spool leg by leg, and the flat pattern to cut from sheet - rather than being an extension somebody sells you.

ZCAD, the other Pascal one

ZCAD is written in Free Pascal and Lazarus, as this is, has been going for years longer, and is worked on daily. The two hardly overlap, which is the useful thing to know: they are different tools that happen to share a language.

What we do not have

MissingWhere to get it
Groups and componentsGroups are built - see Groups. Components, a definition with copies that all change together, are not; a copy of a group here is a group of its own.
Reading other people's filesIts own format only. DXF import is planned, and last; DWG is not planned. LibreCAD, QCAD and ZCAD read both.
LayersGroups organize by what a thing is; nothing here organizes by kind of line, or hides a kind. Every drafting program has layers and SketchUp has tags.
Solid union and intersectionA shape can be drilled through a solid and that is all. Plasticity, FreeCAD, SolveSpace, Dune 3D, Tinkercad and paid SketchUp do the rest.
Rounded edges in 3DThe arc tool fillets a flat corner. Plasticity and Shapr3D are built round doing it to a solid.
Hatching, linetypes, paper layoutsLibreCAD, QCAD, ZCAD.
Shadows and a perspective viewThe light follows the camera, as SketchUp's does; shadows and a perspective camera for looking are planned.
Other languagesEnglish only. LibreCAD is in over thirty.
Parametric historyGoing back and changing a number afterwards: FreeCAD, SolveSpace, Dune 3D. A shape roughed out here exports to OpenSCAD, and can be made parametric there.
A plugin languageSketchUp's Ruby, FreeCAD's and Blender's Python, QCAD's JavaScript, ZCAD's Pascal Script. Here there is no API: the drawing is text - Heck, experimental - and a jig is any program that prints it, in whatever language is already on the machine.
Photo-real renderingNot attempted, and not planned.
BIM, assemblies, FEA, CAMRevit and FreeCAD.
A macOS buildThe code should build on it; nobody has built it.
