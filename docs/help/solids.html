Solids and 3D printing

Why a shape does or does not print.

  • A shape prints when it is closed - every edge shared by exactly two faces, so there is a definite inside.
  • The export tells you: closed and ready to slice, or that a slicer will have to guess.
  • A face pointing the wrong way is drawn in the back-face color. /holes draws every edge that nothing meets in red, over the model, so you can see where it is open rather than only that it is - it checks what you have selected, or every solid if nothing is. /rebuild settles loose faces against their neighbors.
  • /center, or Center on the Origin on the right button, puts a selection on 0,0,0, which is where a slicer expects it.
  • /tozero, or Into the Corner at 0,0,0, puts the near bottom corner there instead, so the thing stands on the floor with its edges against zero - which is what you want when you are measuring rather than printing.
