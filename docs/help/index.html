The tools

Select

Pick what you want to work on.

Line

Draw a line, or a chain of them.

Rectangle

Two corners, and it makes a face.

Circle

A circle from the middle out.

Arc

An arc between two points.

Push/Pull

Lift a face into a solid.

Revolve

Spin a face round an axis into a solid.

Move

Move things, or copy them.

Rotate

Turn things about a point.

Offset

A parallel copy of a face's edge, in or out.

Drill

Push a shape right through everything.

Erase

Delete things.

Measure

Read a distance.

Dimension

Put a measurement on the drawing.

Protractor

Lay a guide line at an angle.

Text

A note on the drawing.

Orbit

Get about the model.
