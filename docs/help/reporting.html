When it breaks

Telling us about it.

Sending one

  1. /report.
  2. Say what you were doing and what happened. A line or two is plenty.
  3. Send.

Nothing leaves the machine until you press send.

What goes with it

  • A picture of the screen as it is now.
  • The drawing itself, so the fault can be reproduced rather than imagined. Untick that if you would rather not send your work - the rest still helps.
  • The last few dozen things you did, with the tool, the view and where you clicked.
  • How the frames have been going: how many took longer than a fortieth of a second, the worst one, and which part of the frame was slow. So "it felt glitchy" arrives with the numbers already in it.
  • What machine this is - memory, processor, graphics, operating system. Nothing about you.

What makes a report easy to act on

  • Send it while it is on the screen. The picture and the last few dozen actions are the most useful part, and they are gone once you carry on.
  • Say what you expected. "It should have snapped to the guide" says more than "snapping is broken", because it says which of the several things it might have snapped to you meant.
  • One fault per report where you can. Two in one are two things to find and one place to say they were fixed.
