Typing measurements

You can type instead of dragging, nearly everywhere.

  • Feet and inches: 12', 8", 12'6", 12' 6 1/2".
  • Plain numbers are in whatever units the drawing is in.
  • A rectangle takes 12'x8'.
  • An angle takes 34.1, or 8:12 for a slope.
  • With the circle or arc tool live, a number with an s on it - 24s - sets how many sides or segments it is drawn with. + and - do the same one at a time.
  • A slash starts a command instead of a measurement - see commands.
