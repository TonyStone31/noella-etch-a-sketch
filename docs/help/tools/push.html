Push/Pull

Lift a face into a solid.

Keyboard: P

How to use it

  1. Click a face and type how far to lift it, or drag it.
  2. A loop of lines has to be closed before it is a face you can push.
  3. Pull it back into itself to cut a shape away.

A face with a window in it

A rectangle drawn inside another leaves a ring: an outline with a window in it, the window belonging to the face inside. Pointing at the ring stipples the ring only, the reading in the top right is the area of the ring, and pushing it raises a wall with the opening lined - not a filled block.
