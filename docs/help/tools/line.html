Line

Draw a line, or a chain of them.

Keyboard: L

How to use it

  1. Click where it starts, then click the end - or just type a length and press Enter.
  2. In a 3D view the arrow keys lock the plane first: left or right stands it up, up or down lays it flat, Esc lets go.
  3. A locked plane holds every point of the shape to it, which is how you draw a shape on one face without it wandering.
  4. Close a loop and the middle becomes a face.
