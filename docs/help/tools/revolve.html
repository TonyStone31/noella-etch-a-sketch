Revolve

Spin a face round an axis into a solid.

How to use it

  1. Draw the outline of half of it - the profile.
  2. Click the face, then click two points on the axis to spin about.
  3. Or click a circle and it follows that round instead.
  4. Type an angle first for a part turn.
  5. This is SketchUp's Follow Me, and it will also sweep a face along a line.
