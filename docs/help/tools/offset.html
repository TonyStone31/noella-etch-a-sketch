Offset

A parallel copy of a face's edge, in or out.

Keyboard: F

How to use it

  1. Click a face, then move in or out and click - or type a wall thickness.
  2. The corners stay sharp and the spacing stays exact, which is what makes it a wall and not a shrunk copy.
